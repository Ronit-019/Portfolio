## Technical Philosophy
- **Modularity**: Code should be highly decoupled, clean, and testable. No clutter, no unnecessary wrappers.
- **Problem-First**: AI is a tool to solve business problems. Focus on latency, cost, and user trust over just chasing theoretical metric accuracies.
- **Systems Thinking**: Designing architectures with failure states, analytics, and fallback layers in mind (e.g. implementing local vector fallbacks for cloud databases).
